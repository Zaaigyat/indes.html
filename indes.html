Discover what drives you.

This informal interest quiz explores six career-interest areas. Answer honestly based on what you enjoy, not what you think you should choose.

What is RIASEC?

RIASEC stands for Realistic, Investigative, Artistic, Social, Enterprising, and Conventional. These categories can help you explore work environments that match your interests.

This quiz is for exploration only. It is not a validated psychological assessment or a guarantee of career success.

🌿 OUR STORY

Helping people grow, one step at a time.

AcadWorks is a career-discovery website concept designed to make career exploration and job-search preparation easier to approach.

🎯

Our Mission

To help students and job seekers explore their interests, learn about careers, and find useful resources for preparing for work.

🌤️

Our Vision

A future where more people can access career information, develop useful skills, and make informed decisions about their working lives.

💚 WHY ACADWORKS?

Career exploration should feel less overwhelming.

Choosing a career can be difficult, especially for students who are still discovering their strengths and interests. AcadWorks brings together introductory career tools, job-search examples, and practical guides in one approachable place.

This is an educational prototype. Its example listings are fictional, and its contact form does not send messages to a server.

SDG 8: Decent Work and Economic Growth.

Promote sustained, inclusive and sustainable economic growth, full and productive employment, and decent work for all.

🌱 OUR CONNECTION

Opportunity matters.

Access to career information and job-search preparation can help people make informed decisions. AcadWorks is designed to support learning about employment opportunities and the importance of decent work.

Official UN Goal 8 ↗
🌱

What does SDG 8 focus on?

Decent work means more than simply having a job. It includes safe working conditions, fair treatment, rights at work, and opportunities to earn a living.

💼

Productive employment

Support opportunities for people to develop skills and participate in meaningful work.

⚖️

Fair work

Promote safe workplaces, equal opportunity, and respect for workers' rights.

📈

Sustainable growth

Encourage economic progress that can benefit communities over time.

🧑‍🎓

Youth opportunities

Help young people access career information, learning, and pathways toward employment.

How AcadWorks connects to SDG 8

The website supports awareness and preparation by offering career-interest exploration, job-search guidance, workplace-safety information, and links to external employment resources. It does not directly provide employment or guarantee job placement.
